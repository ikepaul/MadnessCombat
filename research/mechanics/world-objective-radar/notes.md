# Target tracker (objective radar)

**Category:** world / UI · **Seen in:** ep2 0:43–0:45.4, ep2 1:00–1:02.4 · **Confidence:** high

Clips (local only): `clip-1` the radar screen shows a red blip at the top edge · `clip-2` the blip is dead centre = target right below.

## What happens on screen
- Handheld device: circular radar with rings and a crosshair. A **red blip** shows the target's direction and distance. Centred blip = you're right on top of him, so the hero blasts through the roof.

## Game translation
- Stage 2 (and optionally 3) HUD element: a small radar showing the direction of the final target relative to the current room. Since rooms are fixed screens, it mostly helps pick between exits (left/right/up/down/vent).
- A centred blip can also mark a breach point (`world-breach`).
