# Last stand (scripted ending)

**Category:** world / ending · **Seen in:** ep3 3:40.2–3:47.8 (and ep2's ending at 4:00, where the hero is simply shot from behind) · **Confidence:** high

Clips (local only): `clip-1` the screen bars go red. The halo boss stabs the hero through the chest from behind. The hero fires the shotgun backwards over his shoulder without turning and kills him, kills the target, then dies.

## What happens on screen
- Both stage endings are **scripted ambushes from behind** by the halo boss:
  - ep2: shot in the back of the head while holding the target at gunpoint. The hero dies, end card "Moral: Don't try to shoot the [target]".
  - ep3: impaled, but gets a few seconds to take both the boss and the target with him, then dies. Red bars = "dying" state.

## Game translation
- Ep2 ending: non-interactive cutscene after the target surrenders.
- Ep3 ending: a short **playable last stand**: red bars, the player can't move but can aim and fire for ≈ 5 s (invented). Killing the boss (behind you) and the target finishes the stage. A gun pointed backwards over the shoulder is allowed here only.
- Both endings make "the player dies at the end" part of the stage rather than a game over.
