## What happens on screen
Disarming is the signature Madness move. Five variants appear:
1. **Steal**: grab the enemy's gun at contact range, twist it away, now it's yours (and you shoot them with it).
2. **Knock away**: shove/kick the weapon arm, the weapon spins up into the air and lands nearby.
3. **Shoot it out of the hand**: a shot at the weapon sends it flying. The enemy is left unarmed and surrenders.
4. **Thrown weapon to the hand/head**: the enemy drops the gun.
5. **Grab a melee weapon** mid-swing (street sign) and pull it out of their hands.

## Game translation
- `Grab` at contact range on an armed enemy = **steal** (0.4 s, invented): you get their weapon, they're unarmed. Works on grunts and the clown. Bosses only at scripted openings (proposal).
- Melee hits on the weapon arm have a chance to knock the weapon away (invented: 100% for kicks/sweeps, 30% for punches).
- Free aim makes "shoot it out of the hand" possible: a hitscan ray that crosses the weapon sprite before the body disarms instead of killing (small hitbox, needs precision).
- Unarmed grunts then look for a floor weapon or punch.
