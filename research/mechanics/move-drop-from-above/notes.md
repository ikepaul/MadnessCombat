# Drop from above (vent / ceiling)

**Category:** player movement / stealth entry · **Seen in:** ep3 0:20.3–0:24.0 (vent drop, double-knife kill), ep2 3:29.7–3:31.6 (vent behind guards in cover), ep2 1:03–1:05 (through a blasted ceiling) · **Confidence:** high

Clips (local only): `clip-1` ep3 vent grate opens, hero drops behind the guard and stabs him with both knives · `clip-2` ep2 guards are dug in behind a crate facing the lift; the hero drops from a ceiling vent behind them.

## What happens on screen
- Vents/holes in the ceiling are an alternative entrance into a room, **behind** enemies who are facing the normal door.
- Landing on/next to an unaware enemy goes straight into a kill.

## Game translation
- Room entrances can be marked `vent` (enter from the ceiling at a chosen x). Enemies placed facing the main door are "unaware" of a vent entry, so it triggers the contextual takedown (see `melee-stealth-kill`).
- In a room flow, the vent route is the reward for exploring: the same room has a frontal (door, enemies behind cover) and a flanking (vent) approach.
- Drop = fall from the top of the screen, land with a 0.15 s crouch, no damage.
