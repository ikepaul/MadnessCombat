## Game translation
- New kind `agent`. Character sheet first, in our own design (do not copy the show's suit-and-shades look beyond "formal and dark").
- **12 hp** (invented; grunts have 4). Bullets: head 4 / body 2 as usual, so 3 headshots. **Light punches don't stun him** (heavies and weapon hits do). He blocks the first light punch of a chain (invented).
- Behaviour: comes to melee (takes an attack token like an unarmed grunt) but fires the big pistol (0.4 s aim telegraph, like gun grunts) when the player is more than 3 H away. Walks at the hero's speed.
- **Blunt weakness** (invented, from the baton kill): baton/pipe/bat hits do ×2 to him, so the room should have one on the floor.
- Drops a big pistol (new heavy pistol: slow fire, every hit staggers) on death.
- Ep4 has one; later stages can use him as the elite of a wave.
