# Decapitation & heads as projectiles

**Category:** melee / gore · **Seen in:** ep1 0:52.5–0:55.5 (zombie's head knocked off, body keeps standing), ep1 1:03.8–1:05 (punch knocks a zombie's head across the screen) · **Confidence:** high

Clips (local only): `clip-1` zombie loses its head but stays standing until shot · `clip-2` punch sends a head flying toward the boss.

## What happens on screen
- Strong hits can **knock heads off**. The head flies as a separate object.
- Zombies keep fighting/standing **headless** until shot.
- The wiki lists "severed head" as a weapon in eps 1 and 3 (thrown by a grunt).

## Game translation
- Gore: heads are separate physics parts. A head knocked off a grunt = instant kill. On a zombie it's not a kill (see `enemy-zombie`).
- Loose heads can be picked up and thrown like any small object (light knockback, no kill). Mostly flavour.
- Since the art is our own, "head" = the round top shape of our character design.
