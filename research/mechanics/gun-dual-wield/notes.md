# Dual wield & split aim

**Category:** guns · **Seen in:** ep1 1:25–1:37 (SMG + sawed-off, then two PPKs firing in opposite directions from the cannon), ep2 3:07–3:14 (two shotguns, one each way), ep2 3:47–3:58 (two PPKs at the target) · **Confidence:** high

Clips (local only): `clip-1` ep1 standing on the cannon, one pistol pointed left, one right · `clip-2` ep2 stepping out of the lift firing two shotguns at two different grunts.

## What happens on screen
- The hero holds **two guns, even of different types** (SMG + sawed-off, two shotguns).
- He aims each arm **independently**: one arm left, one arm right, both firing at once.

## Game translation (decided: free mouse aim)
- Two hands. Picking up a one-handed gun while holding one = dual wield. Two-handed long guns can be dual-wielded as in the show (proposal: allowed, with more spread).
- Default: **both arms aim at the cursor**. Left mouse = left hand, right mouse = right hand (or both on one button).
- **Split aim**: hold a key and the second arm locks onto the nearest enemy on the opposite side of the body (or stays at its last angle). That gives the show's "one gun each way" without needing a second cursor.
- Reloading while dual-wielding isn't shown. Proposal: dual-wielded guns can't be reloaded, only thrown when empty (see `gun-empty-throw`).

## Invented / not shown
- Control mapping, auto-lock for the off hand.
