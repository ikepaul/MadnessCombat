# Reload

**Category:** guns · **Seen in:** ep2 0:53.8–0:56.2 (pistol reload on the roof) · **Confidence:** medium (wide shot, small figure)

Clips (local only): `clip-1` hero tilts the pistol up, the empty magazine drops (stays on the floor), new mag in, slide racked.

## What happens on screen
- The only on-screen reload in eps 1–3. Everywhere else the hero **throws empty guns away and picks up new ones** (see `gun-empty-throw`), which is the more Madness-like behaviour.
- The dropped magazine stays on the ground.

## Game translation
- Reload exists but is secondary: you only carry the mag in the gun plus **at most one spare** (invented). Most of the time the fast option is to throw the empty gun and grab one off a corpse.
- Pistol reload 0.6 s. Rifle/SMG 1.0 s (invented). Pump shotgun loads shell by shell, 0.4 s each (invented).
- Spent magazines spawn as floor debris.
