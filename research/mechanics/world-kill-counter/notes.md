# Kill counter HUD

**Category:** world / UI · **Seen in:** ep3 (whole episode, bottom-right) · **Confidence:** high

Clips (local only): `clip-1` the counter appears at the first kill · `clip-2` the counter turns green for zombie kills.

## What happens on screen
- Big number in the bottom-right corner. It appears at the first kill and goes up with every death the hero causes.
- **Green while zombies are being killed**, white for normal kills. Ends at 90 (the wiki counts 91; the animator missed some).
- Every episode ends with a text card stating the body count (ep1: "thirty men... thirty two if you count the zombies", ep2: "Body count: 80").

## Game translation
- Kill counter HUD on every stage (proposal), styled our own way. Colour changes for zombie kills.
- End-of-stage card: body count vs. the episode's count (32 / 86 / 91) as a fun target/score.
