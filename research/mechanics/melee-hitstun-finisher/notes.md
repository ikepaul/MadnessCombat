# Hit-stun & finisher on a downed enemy

**Category:** melee · **Seen in:** ep1 0:37.2–0:40.0 (f1116–f1200), ep2 1:45–1:48 · **Confidence:** medium

Clips (local only): `clip-1` punch to the face (impact star) leaves the grunt standing, stunned, then the hero finishes him and hits him again on the ground.

## What happens on screen
- A punch to the face sometimes **doesn't drop** the grunt: he stays standing, stunned, for about 0.7 s.
- The hero follows up with a second hit that drops him, then **hits the downed enemy** on the ground to make sure.
- ep1 also has the hero blowing up the pile of bodies with a grenade "to make sure they're dead" (wiki).

## Game translation
- Hit-stun state: a light hit stuns 0.7 s, during which the next hit is guaranteed.
- Downed enemies (knocked down but not dead, e.g. tougher enemies or zombies) can be hit on the ground with a downward attack.
- A "make sure" mechanic pairs naturally with zombies: corpses can be raised by the halo boss, so destroying corpses (grenade gibs) is a real tactic in stages with the halo boss.
