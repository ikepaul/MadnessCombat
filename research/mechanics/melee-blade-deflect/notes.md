# Blade deflects bullets (and a gun turns a blade)

**Category:** melee · **Seen in:** ep4 2:45.8–2:48.2 (bullets), 1:46–1:47 (gun turns a sword) · **Confidence:** high for the bullet deflect (frames + wiki), low for the gun block

Clips (local only): `clip-1` the hero, out of ammo, holds a sword up while the zombie agent fires a burst: sparks on the blade, then one round gets through. `clip-2` close-up: a sword swings down, the hero knocks it aside with his SMG and shoots.

## What happens on screen
- Sword held diagonally in front of the body; the burst's tracers end in sparks on the blade. After several deflections one bullet gets through and drops the hero.
- In the vending room close-ups the hero turns a sword aside with the body of his SMG, then fires at contact range.

## Game translation
- **Guard** with a sword-class weapon (sword, katana, scimitar): hold RMB (long weapons keep RMB = thrust; swords get guard). While guarding, bullets from the front within a 70° cone hit the blade (spark + ricochet tick) instead of you, **one in four gets through** (invented; the show lets one through), and you walk at 40%.
- **Parry** (invented, the upgrade): pressing guard within 0.15 s before a hit (bullet or blade) reflects it: a bullet goes back along its ray and can hit the shooter; a blade swing staggers the attacker (a chain hit for the M10b combos).
- **Gun block** (low confidence): a melee swing that reaches a hero holding a gun, while he presses F within 0.2 s, is turned aside and the attacker is stunned at contact range (a point-blank shot opportunity).

## Invented / not shown
- The cone, the leak ratio, the parry window, the gun-block input.
