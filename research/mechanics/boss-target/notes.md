## What happens on screen
- Not a fighter. He's the **objective the hero chases** through the whole stage:
  - Flees whenever the hero enters, firing a wild shot (that kills his own man).
  - At the end: hides behind cover, pops up with a weak pistol, gets disarmed, sweats, surrenders.
  - Uses **level-wide triggers**: watches security cameras and activates the Improbability Drive (ep3).
- The tracker in ep2 points at him (see `world-objective-radar`).

## Game translation
- `Target` NPC: appears in transition rooms, always running toward the next door. Can't be killed until the final room (he's gone before you can line up a shot, or bodyguards block).
- Final room: desk-cover peek-and-shoot pattern (1 shot every ~1.5 s, invented). Shoot-to-disarm → surrender → stage-end cutscene.
- Ep3: security-camera rooms are where he can "see" you. The Improbability Drive switch happens in a cutscene after the camera room.
