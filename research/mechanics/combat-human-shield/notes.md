# Human shield

**Category:** combat · **Seen in:** ep3 1:28–1:32.8 (clown's machine gun) · **Confidence:** high

Clips (local only): `clip-1` the jetpack clown opens up with the MG, the hero holds a grunt in front of himself, the body soaks the whole burst and explodes in gore, the clown runs dry.

## What happens on screen
- The hero holds a body (live grunt, killed by the clown's fire) between himself and the gunfire. The shield absorbs every bullet.
- Once the gun is empty the hero drops the shield and arms himself.

## Game translation
- From `grab`: holding an enemy (alive or dead) makes them a shield on the side you're facing. Hitscan rays from that side hit the shield instead.
- Shield durability: a body absorbs ~30 hits (invented to survive one MG burst) before it falls apart. A live grunt shield dies on the first hit but keeps blocking.
- You move slower while holding (0.6×, invented) and can fire a one-handed gun with the free hand.
- Throw the shield at any time (see `melee-grab-throw`).
