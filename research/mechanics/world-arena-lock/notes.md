**Category:** world / level event · **Seen in:** ep3 2:24.6–2:28.4 (desert) · **Confidence:** high

Clips (local only): `clip-1` on open ground, walls slam down from above one at a time (right, back, left) until the fight is sealed in a box with no doors. A grunt caught under a wall dies.

## What happens on screen
- The open area turns into a **closed room with no exits**. Later waves drop in from above, then the halo boss raises the corpses.
- The only way out is getting knocked through a wall by the boss.

## Game translation
- Room event: "lock-in" walls drop with shadow telegraphs (≈ 0.5 s each) and crush anything under them.
- A sealed room = survival wave room: enemies spawn from above, the exit appears only when a scripted event happens (here: the boss blasts the player through a wall into the next room).
