# Melee weapons (baton, knife, axe, sword, bat, pipe, scimitar)

**Category:** melee / weapons · **Seen in:** ep2 1:06–1:12 (batons), ep2 2:57–3:05 (knives/swords), ep3 0:45–1:12 (knives, axe, baton, bat) · **Confidence:** high

Clips (local only): `clip-1` ep2 baton brawl in the office · `clip-2` ep3 axe + baton room (axe lodges in a head, axe thrown at a grunt around the corner).

## What happens on screen
- Grunts carry **batons** (ep2, they look like police), **knives, swords, scimitars, axes, bats, pipes** (ep3).
- The hero takes melee weapons off the floor or knocks them out of enemies' hands, then uses them. One hit usually kills.
- Melee weapons can be **thrown**: the axe at a grunt coming around a corner (ep3 f1800–f1930), knives constantly.
- An axe can get **stuck in a head** and has to come free.

## Game translation

- Weapons have no durability in the show. Proposal: none, but they drop on knockdown.
