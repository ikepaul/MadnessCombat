# Welder (plasma cutter)

**Category:** enemy · **Seen in:** ep2 2:24.7–2:31 (elevator roof) · **Confidence:** high

Clips (local only): `clip-1` a welder in a welding mask cuts a slot in the elevator roof (sparks) so the SMG grunt can fire down through it. The welder is killed by the gunner's burst/the hero and falls through into the cab.

## What happens on screen
- Unique grunt: welding mask, plasma cutter. Doesn't fight directly. **Cuts an opening** in a surface (the cab roof) that other enemies then shoot/drop through.
- Dies like a normal grunt.

## Game translation
- Support enemy: goes to a marked `cuttable` surface and spends ~3 s (f4410–f4490 ≈ 2.7 s) cutting a hole, which opens a new attack angle/spawn point.
- Killing the welder before the cut finishes stops the hole. That makes him a priority target.
- Use only in set pieces (elevator) like the show.
