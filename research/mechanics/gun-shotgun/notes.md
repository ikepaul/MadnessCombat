# Shotguns (sawed-off, pump)

**Category:** guns · **Seen in:** ep1 1:27–1:28.6 (sawed-off), ep2 1:26–1:41 (pump: point blank and across the corridor), ep3 3:38–3:45 (pump, final room) · **Confidence:** high

Clips (local only): `clip-1` ep1 sawed-off point-blank kill · `clip-2` ep2 pump shotgun across the corridor.

## What happens on screen
- Big white muzzle burst + **several parallel horizontal pellet streaks** (not a wide cone).
- Point blank: the head bursts, heavy blood spray on the wall behind.
- Kills at range too (across a whole room).
- The ep1 sawed-off fires 3 times without reloading (wiki notes this as an animation error).

## Game translation
- Shotgun = 5–6 hitscan rays with a narrow spread (±4°, invented to match the near-parallel streaks). Kills anything in the cluster. High knockback at close range (bodies fly back).
- Pump: 0.6 s between shots (invented), 6 shells. Sawed-off: 2 shots then reload/throw (follow the real gun, not the error).
- Point-blank shotgun on a head = gib (head burst) gore effect.
