# Catch a thrown weapon and throw it back

**Category:** throwing / skill move · **Seen in:** ep3 3:11.5–3:14.9 (vending machine room) · **Confidence:** high (wiki confirms)

Clips (local only): `clip-1` a grunt throws a knife at the hero, the hero catches it and throws it into another grunt. Then a grunt finds the hero's discarded pistol empty and gets thrown into the vending machine.

## What happens on screen
- Enemy knife thrown at the hero → hero **catches it out of the air** → throws it straight back, killing a grunt.

## Game translation
- Timed parry: pressing `grab` (or melee) with an empty hand within a short window (≈ 0.15 s, invented) before a thrown object hits = catch it. You're then holding that weapon and can throw it back.
- Works on thrown knives, axes and guns. Not on bullets.
- Enemy throwers: some grunts throw their knife when they're > 2 H away (ep3 behaviour).
