# Breaching through the floor/roof

**Category:** world / level design · **Seen in:** ep2 1:02.8–1:06 · **Confidence:** high

Clips (local only): `clip-1` the hero drops a grenade at his feet on the roof and steps back. Inside, the ceiling blows open and he drops into the target's office among the guards.

## What happens on screen
- A grenade on a marked spot blows a hole through the floor. The hero drops through into the room below, **surprising** everyone (the target flees, guards scramble).
- The scorch hole stays in the ceiling.

## Game translation
- `breachable` floor/ceiling tiles: an explosion on them opens a hole = a room transition that enters the next room from above.
- Entering by breach starts the room with enemies "surprised" (≈ 1 s before they react, invented). It rewards the alternate route the same way vents do.
