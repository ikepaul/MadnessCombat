# Throwing knives (stick, retrieve)

**Category:** throwing · **Seen in:** ep3 0:23.5–0:31.8 (knife throws across the room, then pulls the knives back out), ep1 1:29–1:30 (knife sticks in a grunt's head), ep3 0:45–0:53 (throws mid-melee), ep3 2:12 (knife disarms the squad leader) · **Confidence:** high (measured)

Clips (local only): `clip-1` ep3 two throws, two kills, the hero walks over and pulls both knives out · `clip-2` ep1 thrown knife sticks in a grunt's head.

## What happens on screen
- A thrown knife **flies almost instantly** and **sticks** in the target (usually the head). One-hit kill.
- The knife stays in the corpse. The hero **walks over and pulls it out** to reuse it.
- He carries two knives, stabs with them at contact range and throws them at range.

## Game translation
- Knives are a **projectile** (not hitscan): speed 13 H/s, slight drop, one-hit kill, stick into whatever they hit (corpse or wall).
- Stuck knives are pickups at the spot they landed: walking over the corpse picks them back up.
- Dual knives: left/right hand each throw one. Melee stab when the cursor is within 0.6 H.
