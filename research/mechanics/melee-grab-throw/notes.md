# Grab & throw enemies / bodies

**Category:** melee · **Seen in:** ep1 0:24 & 0:43 (hurls grunts onto the pile), ep2 1:12–1:21 (throws bodies across the room into doorways), ep3 3:13 (throws a grunt into the vending machine), ep3 2:16 (throws a grunt overhead) · **Confidence:** high

## What happens on screen
- The hero grabs an enemy at contact range and throws them **hard, at head height**, across the room. They hit walls/doorways/other enemies and drop.
- Throws are also used to **move corpses** (piling them up).
- Throwing someone into an object (vending machine) kills them.

## Game translation
- `Grab` at contact range (same depth band). While holding: throw in the aim direction (free aim, so you can throw across depth too). Thrown bodies are projectiles that **knock down anything they hit** and kill on wall impact at high speed.
- A grabbed live enemy can instead be held as a **human shield** (see `combat-human-shield`).
- Corpses can be grabbed and thrown too (used to clear doorways, or to use as a shield as in ep3).
