## Game translation
- Some level props are **pickup-able weapons** (sign posts, pipes). Pick up = short pull animation (≈ 0.5 s).
- Long weapons: reach 1.5 H, multi-hit sweep (hits everything in the arc), slow (≈ 0.5 s swing).
- `Pin` finisher: a thrust into an enemy standing next to a wall/solid prop sticks them there. The weapon stays in the body, so you have to pick a new one.
