# Prone dodge & prone fire

**Category:** player movement · **Seen in:** ep1 1:20.5–1:24.3 (f2415–f2525) · **Confidence:** high

Clips (local only): `clip-1` drops flat under a cannonball, gets up, dives again, shoots the boss from the ground, kips up.

## What happens on screen
- The hero throws himself flat on the ground and the cannonball (chest height) flies over.
- He stays prone, **draws the SMG and fires while lying down**. The shot goes over the cannon into the boss's head.
- Gets back up with a quick roll/kip.

## Game translation
- `Dive/prone` button: very fast drop (0.15 s), hitbox shrinks to ≈ 0.3 H tall, so head- and chest-height projectiles pass over. You can aim and shoot while prone (free aim, arm pivots from the shoulder).
- You can't walk while prone. Proposal: allow a slow crawl (invented) or none. Default none.
- Standing up takes 0.4 s. Pressing dive while moving = forward dive (≈ 1 H forward) as at f2460.
