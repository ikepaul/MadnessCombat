# Stage outlines (one stage per episode)

Each episode broken into fixed-screen rooms (decided layout), with the enemies, events and mechanics it introduces. Times point into the beat logs in `episodes/`. Enemy numbers are approximate counts from the footage and the wiki body counts, and serve as an upper bound for wave design.

---

## Stage 1 (episode 1, 2:07): one outdoor arena
**Room:** a single flat outdoor screen with one tree. Enemies enter from both screen edges. Everything happens in this one room.
**Starting loadout:** nothing (fists).
**Teaches:** walk, punch, knockdown/get-up, grab & throw, grenade, zombies, jump/prone dodges, dual wield, standing on objects.

| # | Beat (episode time) | Gameplay |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intro: boombox NPC by the tree, dancer NPC in the background (0:16) | Walk up to the boombox → scripted punch knockdown (teaches get-up) → kill the boombox NPC (melee tutorial, low sweep). |
| 2 | Unarmed riot (0:21–0:44) | **Wave A**: ≈ 18 unarmed grunts trickling in from both edges, 1–2 at a time. Bodies pile up. Grab & throw onto the pile. |
| 3 | Grenade (0:44–0:48) | Scripted grenade pickup → blow up the body pile (corpse gibbing matters next). |
| 4 | Halo boss arrives (0:47–1:24) | **Boss phase 1**: descends, raises 2 zombies with lightning (from surviving corpses), SMG pickup appears, cross shield blocks fire, lightning knockdown + disarm, hover-dash grab + body slams. **Phase 2**: sweeps corpses away, summons the cannon (shadow drop), cannon volleys (jump / prone), dies to a shot while not shielded (prone shot under the cannon). |
| 5 | Armed finale (1:25–1:38) | **Wave B**: ≈ 9 grunts. Hero has SMG + sawed-off, knives, 2 pistols (pickups). One grunt mans the cannon. Jump onto the cannon, split-aim dual pistols both ways. |
| 6 | Ending (1:40–2:07) | Pick up the boombox, (optionally) shoot the dancer, dance emote, body-count card (target 32). |

**Enemy budget:** 27 grunts, 2 zombies, 1 boss, 2 NPCs.

## Stage 2 (episode 2: Redeemer, 4:06): infiltration to the target
**Starting loadout:** fiber wire + suppressed pistol. **Objective:** reach the target, guided by the tracker.
**Teaches:** stealth takedowns, cover, tracker, breach, door waves, disarm-steal, zombie horde, elevator set piece, climbing, interrogation, shoot-to-disarm.

| # | Room (episode time) | Gameplay |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Outside wall (0:12–0:27) | 1 smoking guard, unaware → wire takedown from behind. (Manhole: flavour.) |
| 2 | Building corner + ladder (0:28–0:38) | 1 guard. Peek around the corner and suppressed headshot. Climb the ladder, vault the parapet (transition). |
| 3 | Rooftop A (0:38–0:48) | 2 SMG guards beside a box → slip behind the box (cover), lean out, headshots. First tracker check. |
| 4 | Rooftop B, roof door (0:49–1:03) | 3 guards (1 unarmed) → takedown + quick shots, reload. Tracker blip centred → drop a grenade = **breach**. |
| 5 | Target's office (1:03–1:38) | Enter from the ceiling. The target flees left (his shot kills his own guard). **Door waves** from both doors: baton + pistol grunts, ≈ 15–20. Pump shotgun pickup. Halo boss peeks in at the end (stalking). |
| 6 | Corridor (1:39–2:19) | Shotgun grunts, close-quarters disarms. **Clown + squad** arrive: steal his rifle. **Halo boss** flings you across the room and mass-raises **8 zombies** → rifle vs. the horde. Boss leaves. More grunts from the left door. |
| 7 | **Elevator** (2:20–2:53) | Set piece (see `mechanics/world-elevator-fight`): roof attackers, welder, gunner through the slot, grenade throw-back, drops through the hole, door ambush, climb onto the roof, shoot down, shoot the cables. Climb the cable up. |
| 8 | Sword room (2:54–3:10) | Knife/sword grunts from the left door and a lift. Blade disarms. One grunt left dazed. Pick up a second shotgun → dual shotguns. |
| 9 | Lobby (3:11–3:24) | Step out of the lift firing both ways. Last grunt → **interrogation + knockout**. |
| 10 | Crate room (3:27–3:47) | Guards dug in behind a crate facing the lift. Peek, retreat, then **flank through the ceiling vent**. Waves from the right door and the lift behind you. Pick up 2 pistols. |
| 11 | Final office (3:50–4:00) | Target + 2 bodyguards. Target hides behind the desk, pops up with a pistol → **shoot it out of his hand** → surrender → **ending cutscene**: the halo boss shoots the player from behind. "Moral" card + body count (target 86). |

**Enemy budget:** ≈ 75 grunts (incl. baton, pistol, SMG, shotgun, rifle, knife/sword variants), 1 welder, 8 zombies, clown, 2 bodyguards.

## Stage 3 (episode 3: Avenger, 4:01): revenge run with the Improbability Drive
**Starting loadout:** two knives. **On-screen kill counter** for this stage (the only episode that has one; proposal: all stages).
**Teaches:** knife throwing/retrieval, throwing empty guns, improbability physics, collapsing arena, human shield, long environment weapons, pinning, arena lock, catch-and-return, last stand.

| # | Room (episode time) | Gameplay |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Outside the same building (0:08–0:14) | Cutscene: two guards, one goes inside. (The ep2 vent is boarded over: flavour.) |
| 2 | Vent room (0:14–0:33) | **Drop from the vent** → dual-knife takedown. Two more guards → **knife throws** across the room. Pick up a rifle, **pull the knives back out**. |
| 3 | Table room (0:33–0:57) | Guards + an axe on the floor (enemy grabs it). Waves from the right door. A grunt **flips the table** as cover. Rifle runs dry → **throw it**, jump onto the table, fight from height, then knives again. Pick up the axe. |
| 4 | Camera room (0:57–1:13) | Axe/baton waves, axe thrown around the corner. **Security camera** → cutscene: the target presses the **Improbability Drive** button. |
| 5 | Camera room, improbability mode (1:18–1:33) | Bodies ricochet off walls/ceiling, one sticks in the ceiling. **Room splits, half falls into the void.** **Jetpack clown with MG** over the void → **human shield**. He flies off, re-enters behind you, shoves you into the void. |
| 6 | Bakery street (1:34–2:10) | Fall in from the sky. **Two-sided ambush**, SMG churn (throw empties, grab new ones). Surreal sky. **Giant falling marshmallow** (becomes terrain). Uproot the **street sign** (polearm). **Sun brute** mini-boss. Clown with scimitar → **pin him to the marshmallow** (cutscene: lightning revives him as a zombie). |
| 7 | Desert (2:11–2:48) | Squad leader with shades + knife squad → **knife-disarm** the leader, unarmed brawl. **Walls drop** into a sealed box. Rifle, enemies dropping in from above, blood flood. **Zombie clown** on the jetpack with the street sign, immune to bullets → **grab the sign**, finish him. |
| 8 | Desert box, continued (2:49–3:05) | **Halo boss** drops in, raises ≈ 5 zombies → axe through them. Boss shrugs off pistol fire → **blasts you through the wall** into the next room. |
| 9 | Vending room (3:05–3:24) | Grunts: **catch a thrown knife and throw it back**, enemy grabs your empty pistol (click), throw him into the vending machine. Halo boss flies in through the hole, raises 2 zombies (Tec-9), **sword dive**, shoot him → he **teleports away**. |
| 10 | Console room (3:24–3:38) | The target flees out the right door. Door waves around the console. |
| 11 | Final lift room (3:38–3:48) | Target + **2 elite bodyguards** with automatic weapons. Shotgun them → the halo boss **stabs you from behind** → **last stand**: shoot behind you (boss), kill the target, die. Credits + body count (target 91). |

**Enemy budget:** ≈ 75 grunts (every melee and gun variant, squad leader), 3 elite bodyguards, ≈ 7 zombies, the sun brute, clown ×2 phases, halo boss, the target.

## Stage 4 (episode 4: Apotheosis, 3:18 on YouTube): the club hunt
**Starting loadout:** an SMG and a pistol taken from the car (after the ram). **Objective:** reach the club in the basement and kill the masked DJ. The kill counter stays on (as stage 3).
**Teaches:** vehicle ram, fire-mode switch, the agent, stairs, crowd fighting, blade deflecting bullets, armed zombies, fighting from the ground, the detonator ending.
Beat log: `episodes/ep4-log.md`. Darker look: night outside, dim club (palette 4, own design).

| # | Room (episode time) | Gameplay |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Night alley (0:30–0:45) | Scripted **car ram**: the car crushes two smoking guards against the wall (kill counter 2). Get out, take the SMG + pistol from the car (E at the open door). The building door is the exit. |
| 2 | Poster room (0:45–0:49) | 2 guards at the right door, unaware → quick entry kills. Teaches nothing new; a breather. |
| 3 | Door room (0:49–1:02) | **Door waves** from the right doorway (≈ 8 SMG/pistol grunts). The SMG runs dry → an AK on the pile. **Fire-mode switch** (semi → auto) with a short close-up the first time. |
| 4 | Office (1:02–1:21) | Full-auto AK vs. door waves (≈ 6). **The agent** steps out of the back door: soaks head hits, fights in melee, the baton on the floor is the intended answer. Drops his **big pistol**. |
| 5 | Vending room (1:21–1:52) | Blade waves from both doors (sword, knife, baton, ≈ 12). Thrown bodies **knock the vending machine flat** (it becomes low cover). Take a **sword** from its owner. A grunt picks up a dropped SMG and threatens you (the empty-gun gag from ep3, reversed: this one is loaded). |
| 6 | Stairwell (1:52–2:01) | A **staircase** down to the left: grunts come up it (≈ 5), shot ones **tumble down the steps**. Exit: the dark door at the bottom. |
| 7 | The club, crowd (2:01–2:29) | ≈ 35 **dancers** (unaware, dancing) facing the DJ's stage. Shooting the DJ: he drops, **gets back up** and sends the crowd at you. **Crowd fight**: packed, all sides, sword + SMG. One grunt cowers and smokes in the corner by the right door (doesn't fight). Then the **DJ mini-boss fight** (decided 2026-10-27: he fights and can be killed; his moves are designed in M16). |
| 8 | The club, boss (2:29–2:45) | The right door is **smashed in** (crushes the cowering grunt). The **halo boss** (own design, shades variant) + a **zombie agent with a rifle**. **Mass raise** of every intact corpse (cap ≈ 19). Pistol + sword vs. the horde. |
| 9 | The club, finale (2:45–3:05) | Out of ammo → **deflect** the zombie agent's burst with the sword (block window, chip damage gets through) → the hero is **downed**; from the floor, kill the zombie agent. **Duel with the boss** (sword + pistol). **Ending (decided 2026-10-27)**: beat him and he grabs you as he falls; your grenade goes off and kills you both, then the end card + body count vs. 80. Losing the duel is a normal death (the room restarts). |

**Enemy budget:** ≈ 57 grunts (≈ 22 in rooms 2–6, ≈ 35 dancers), 1 agent, ≈ 19 zombies, 1 zombie agent, the DJ (mini-boss), the halo boss. Biggest risk: 35 dancers + 19 zombies in one room (performance, AI crowding).
