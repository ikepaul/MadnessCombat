## Episode 2 summary for game design
- **Structure**: a chain of small rooms/scenes joined by doors, ladders, lifts and vents — not one arena. Each room is a fight "set-piece" with its own spawn points (doors, lifts, ceiling hole, roof hatch).
- **New for ep2**: stealth kills (wire, suppressed pistol), cover (crate/desk, both sides use it), objective radar, breaching (grenade through floor, drop through vent), elevator fight (enemies attack through the roof), destroying the elevator cables, climbing (ladder, cable), interrogation/knockout, shoot-to-disarm, grenade throw-back, blood + bullet-hole decals, persistent corpses + weapons on the floor, dual shotguns.
- **Enemy roster seen**: unarmed grunt, baton grunt, pistol grunt, SMG grunt, rifle grunt, shotgun grunt, knife/sword grunt, welder (plasma cutter, cuts through ceilings), clown mini-boss (assault rifle, gets disarmed), halo boss (telekinetic throw, mass zombie raise, stalks, ends the stage), target (runs away, hides behind desk, weak pistol, surrenders).
- **Zombies** in ep2 take several hits (2–4) vs. grunts dying from 1.
- **Wiki body count 86**: 75 grunts, 1 welder, 8 zombies, clown, hero.
