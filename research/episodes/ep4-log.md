## Episode 4 summary for game design
- **Structure**: an outdoor arrival (alley + car) → four building rooms joined by doors → a stairwell going down → the club (one big dark room with a stage). The club fight has three phases: crowd → boss arrival + mass raise → duel and ending. Fewer rooms than ep2/ep3, but by far the densest room in the series (wiki: most kills of any room).
- **New for ep4**: vehicle ram entrance (car crushes enemies against a wall), loadout from the car, fire-mode selector, the **agent** (tough suited elite that survives head wounds and drops a big pistol), stairs with bodies tumbling down, a crowd of passive dancers that turns hostile, a boss who ignores a headshot and commands the crowd (non-fighting DJ), a door smashed in that crushes whoever is behind it, an **armed zombie** (zombies were unarmed before), the **blade deflecting bullets**, acting from the ground while downed, the boss using sword + pistol at once, a **detonator ending** (mutual kill).
- **Upgrades to existing mechanics**: zombie raise now works on a whole room of corpses at once (≈ 19, vs ≈ 8 in ep2); thrown bodies knock furniture over (vending machine); sword-heavy melee crowds (blade grunts in every room); the boss carries weapons.
- **Enemy roster seen**: grunts (unarmed, every blade, baton, pipe, pistol, SMG, rifle), dancers (unarmed crowd), the agent, the cowering smoker (doesn't fight), zombies (≈ 19), the zombie agent (rifle), the clown as DJ (non-combatant, bullet-proof mask), the halo boss (shades, sword, big pistol, mass raise).
- **Do-not-copy specifics**: the club's name and posters, the "wanted"/"do not resist" posters, the iron-mask design, the clown's look, the on-screen gag text, the end text, the music. Ours: own club, own DJ look, own HUD gag (or none).
