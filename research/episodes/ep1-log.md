## Episode 1 summary for game design
- **Arena**: one flat screen, enemies enter from both edges, bodies pile up and stay (corpses are persistent objects — they get reused as zombies, stood on, thrown, blown up).
- **Enemy wave order**: unarmed grunts (punch only) → grenade clears bodies → boss enters (lightning/zombies/shield/grab/cannon) → boss killed → armed-ish wave (hero has guns now) → done.
- **Wiki body count 32**: 27 grunts + 2 zombies + boss + boombox guy + dancer.
