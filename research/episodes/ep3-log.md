## Episode 3 summary for game design
- **Structure**: building interior (3 rooms) → Improbability Drive switches on → collapsing room → fall into a surreal street → open desert → walls drop to form a sealed arena → blasted through a wall → vending room → console room → final lift room. Stage ends with a scripted mutual kill.
- **New for ep3**: kill counter HUD, dual-knife stealth drop, throwing knives that you retrieve from corpses, throwing empty guns, flipping tables as cover (enemy), standing on furniture, physics-modifier mode (huge knockback, wall/ceiling bounce, enemies stuck in ceiling), collapsing floor into a void, human shield vs. a machine gun, falling hazard that becomes terrain, uprooted street sign as a long weapon, pinning an enemy to terrain, walls dropping to lock the arena, catching a thrown knife and throwing it back, boss blasting the player through a wall, boss teleport-escape, last-stand "shoot behind you".
- **Enemy roster seen**: grunts with every melee weapon (knife, bat, baton, axe, sword, scimitar, pipe), pistol/SMG/rifle/shotgun grunts, squad leader with shades, elite bodyguards (automatic weapons), zombies (raised by the halo boss; counter shows them in green), clown boss (jetpack + MG → impaled → revived as zombie with street sign, absorbs rifle fire), sun creature (surreal brute), halo boss (raise dead, blast through wall, sword dive, teleport, final ambush), target (flees, presses the Improbability button).
- **Kill counter** reaches 90 on screen (wiki body count 91).
