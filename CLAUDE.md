# Project guide

A browser game where each of the first four episodes of an animated series is one stage (stage 4 added 2026-10-27, milestones M14–M16). We copy **mechanics only** (movement, timings, enemy types, set pieces), never names, character designs, music or text.

- **Plan**: `PLAN.md`. Milestones M0–M13 with checklists and acceptance tests. Work one milestone at a time on a branch `mN-<slug>`, PR to `main`.
- **Research**: `research/`. One note per mechanic (`research/mechanics/<slug>/notes.md`), measurements, stage outlines, design decisions. Local-only reference clips sit next to the notes (gitignored).
- **Look & sound**: `design/art-direction.md`, `design/audio-direction.md`, and the interactive `design/style-frame/` (the renderer is ported from it). The do-not-copy list is in the art direction.
- **Planned next (M17–M20, 2026-10-27)**: `design/qol-pass.md` (quality of life), `design/coop-moves.md` (co-op moves), `design/rage.md` (rage meter, solo and team rage fatalities).

## Commands
Node 26 via nvm (`nvm use`; the system Node is too old for Vite 8).
- `npm run dev`: dev server on :5173 (or `preview_start` "dev" from `.claude/launch.json`; "dev-npm" on :5174 runs it with the system Node, e.g. on Windows without nvm)
- `npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run build`
- Style frame: `preview_start` "style-frame" (:5180)

## Architecture (see PLAN.md "Ground rules")
- `src/sim/`: plain TS, no Phaser. Fixed 60 Hz `FixedClock` (time scale, hit-stop, pause/step) drives `World`. The sim emits typed events (`src/sim/events.ts`); rendering/audio/HUD subscribe.
- Units are **H** (one body height) and seconds. `src/render/camera.ts` `project()` is the only world→screen mapping (measured perspective).
- `src/sim/combat.ts`: actor state machine (free/attack/stun/knockback/down/getUp/grabbed/dead), hits and damage, grabs/throws, sweep, finisher, corpses (tumble → settle → freeze). `src/sim/hurtbox.ts`: head ellipse + body capsule through the pose root.
- `src/anim/`: pose skeleton (part transforms + whole-body root), clips sampled from `src/data/poses.json`, procedural layers. Poses are authored in `/?lab=pose-editor`.
- M6: `src/data/melee.ts` (melee weapons, throwing, grenades, disarm, shield numbers), `src/sim/melee.ts` (swing / thrust + pin / toss / lob / pull / steal actions, grenade cooking, shot-out-of-hand geometry), `src/sim/explosives.ts` (blast, gibs), `src/sim/items.ts` (thrown blades stick in bodies and walls, grenade fuses, catching). Human shield and the light-punch disarm live in `combat.ts`.
- Guns (M5): `src/data/weapons.ts`, `src/anim/aim.ts` (aim rig shared by painter and sim), `src/sim/hitscan.ts` (screen-space rays), `src/sim/guns.ts` (hands, fire, reload, pick-up, thrown guns), `src/render/gunFx.ts` (flash, tracer, casings).
- `src/feel/feel.ts` turns events into hit-stop/shake/chromatic/kill-cam requests (numbers in `src/data/feel.ts`); `src/render/feelFx.ts` carries them out. `src/audio/`: Web Audio mixer (buses, voice limits, reverb, ducking, kill-cam filter), synthesized placeholder sounds (`synth.ts`, ids in `src/data/audio.ts`), music system (`assets/music/cues.json`), event hooks (`hooks.ts`). `src/settings.ts`: volumes + effect intensity.
- `src/render/canvas/`: Canvas 2D vector painters (room, characters, items) drawing into `CanvasLayer`s shown by Phaser. Layers: room (baked) → decals (blood, baked) → corpses (frozen bodies, baked) → actors/props/items (per frame, sorted far→near). `src/render/post.ts`: vignette + grain. HUD/debug in `UIScene` (no post FX).
- M13 shell: `src/settings.ts` (volumes, effect intensity, reduced motion, blood, key bindings), `src/progress.ts` (unlocked stages + room checkpoint, localStorage), `src/ui/` (keyboard/mouse `Menu`, settings and controls items, credits text). Esc in a stage opens the pause menu (`UIScene`); dying restarts the room from its checkpoint.
- Playtest pass (2026-10-27): the hero has his own faster speeds and full air control (`TUNING.move.hero`, `TUNING.jump.hero`, `prone.heroUpTime`; enemies keep the shared ones). Friendly fire for thrown weapons is a setting (`world.friendlyThrow`: damage/knockdown/through). Idles: `src/anim/idles.ts` (guards fidget, `idle: 'chat'` pairs talk, smokers smoke, the hero fidgets after ~3.5 s still; deterministic from time + id). Gore levels: `src/sim/gore.ts` (`StageDef.gore`, stage 3 'extreme': more decaps and gibs, hands off corpses, blood on the killer and his blade; vfx/decals scale up, wounded enemies leave trails).
- M10b combos & fatalities (`TUNING.juggle`, `design/combos-and-fatalities.md`): juggle/spike/wall-bounce/head punt in `src/sim/combat.ts` (`Flight.juggle`, `hang`, `spiked`), pop & catch in `melee.ts` `disarm` + `items.ts` `catchItem` (`primedAt`, `Item.popUntil`), over-the-shoulder in `guns.ts` (`behindContact`, `pose.backShot`); events `juggle`, `wallBounce`, `popUp`, `fatality`. Labs `combo-*`, `fatality-*` (`src/labs/comboLabs.ts`).
- M17 quality of life (`design/qol-pass.md`, `TUNING.qol`, `tests/qol.test.ts`, labs `qol-*` in `src/labs/qolLabs.ts`): `pickupPlan()` / `takePlan()` / `stepUse()` in `guns.ts` (one plan for the sim and the E prompt; smart swap `swapHand`; hold E = `PlayerCommand.useHeld`, `Actor.useHeldAt`), `autoCatch` in `items.ts` (`Item.bounced`, `ownedBy`, `passTo`), the hero's depth band `meleeBand()` in `actor.ts` + `snapTarget()` / depth snap in `combat.ts` (rings via `GameScene.depthRings`), `freeFrontHand` (F with any free hand), cover `coverBetween` in `hitscan.ts` (`castRay(..., muzzle)`), the aim rig's muzzle-to-cursor correction in `aim.ts`, `throwToDepth` in `items.ts`, `lobTarget` in `melee.ts` + `src/render/canvas/markerPainter.ts`.
- M18 co-op moves (`design/coop-moves.md`, `TUNING.coop`, `tests/coopMoves.test.ts`, labs `coop-*` in `src/labs/coopLabs.ts`): `src/sim/coop.ts` (`partnerUnderCursor`: aim at your partner to target them; `tryPass`, `tryLift` / `launchToss` / `setDown`, `stepCarried` / `stepSpin` / `releaseSpin`; `Actor.carried`, `spin`, `tossT`), in `combat.ts` shared chains (`chainBonus`), `doubleTeam`, ping-pong + bat (`Flight.by/pings/lethal/pierce`, `pingPong`, `batAway`, `tryBat`), hold & hit (`heldByPartner`, `heldHit`, `Actor.tender`), the wishbone (`startWishbone` / `tearApart`, `grabbedBy2`, the split render `Pose.cut`, `world.blood`); events `coopMove`, `kill.team / alsoId`, fatality kinds `held | wishbone | double | pingpong`. Partner bot `src/dev/partnerBot.ts`: `/?coop=bot` (&stage=N works) puts an AI player 2 in any stage; labs hand it moves on N (`Lab.partnerTask`).
- M19 rage (`design/rage.md`, `TUNING.rage`, `FEEL.rage`, `tests/rage.test.ts`, labs `rage-*`): `src/sim/rage.ts` (`killType` on every `kill` event, `installRage`: the meter on `Actor.rage`, events `rageGain` / `rageReady`), `src/sim/rageMoves.ts` (`tryRage` on Tab = `PlayerCommand.rage`, `rageVictims`, `startRage`, `stepRageRun`: while `world.rage` is set `World.step` steps only the move, `endRage`; Rampage / Spin shot / Cut line / Home run; events `rageStart` / `rageBeat` / `rageEnd` / `rageFizzle`), `src/ui/rageBar.ts`, the full-rage look (`pose.raging`, red eye slit, wisps), the letterbox and dim (`GameScene.rageDim`, `UIScene.drawLetterbox`), `FeelSink.slowMo`.
- M14 stage 4 mechanics (labs in `src/labs/stage4Labs.ts`, tests `tests/stage4.test.ts`, palette `night`): the agent (`variant: 'agent'`, `TUNING.ai.agent`: 12 hp, blocks the first jab of a chain, blunt x2, brawls inside 3 H, heavy pistol beyond), fire-mode switch (B, `Hand.semi`), sword guard & parry on RMB (`GUARD` in `src/data/melee.ts`, `Actor.guard`, event `block`), stairs (`RoomDef.stairs`, `stairHeight` in `collision.ts`; bodies slide to the bottom), crowds (`idle: 'dance' | 'cower'`, `SpawnOpts.crowd` shares one alarm, `TUNING.ai.crowd` press-in + soft shoves; any hit alerts its target), armed zombies (room flag `armedZombies`), the low blade slash from the ground (`LOW_SLASH`), the gun block (F just before a blade lands, `GUN_BLOCK`), toppleable vending machines (`VendingDef.toppleable`) and the door smash (`src/sim/doorSmash.ts`: `smashDoor`, a `slab` prop).
- `src/data/`: `tuning.ts` (gameplay constants, each citing its research note or "invented"), `feel.ts` (effect numbers), `palettes.ts` (one per stage), rooms.
- Routes: `/` = the title screen (M13, `src/scenes/TitleScene.ts`: Continue, New game, Stage select, Co-op, Settings, Credits); `/?stage=1` = stage 1 from its intro (`src/data/stages/stage1Script.ts`, the whole stage as one sequencer script); `/?wave=A` = straight into the arena's wave A; `/?beat=riot|grenade|boss|waveB|ending` = the stage script from that beat (playtesting; later beats get corpses, the cannon and an SMG); `/?stage=2` = stage 2 room by room, from the last checkpoint (`&room=wall|corner|roofA|roofB|office|corridor|elevator|swords|lobby|crates|final` starts in that room); `/?stage=3` = stage 3 (`&room=outside|vent|tables|camera|street|desert|vending|console|lift`); the end cards chain stage 1 -> 2 -> 3 (Enter: next stage, after stage 3 the credits; R: replay; Esc: title); `/?coop=host` / `/?coop=join` = co-op (`&stage=2` works there too); `/?coop=bot` = co-op with the partner bot as player 2 (M18, dev); `/?room=test` = the M0 style-frame room.
- `src/sim/ai.ts` grunt AI (attack tokens for melee; gun grunts keep 3-5 H, 0.4 s aim telegraph, friendly fire, scavenging, one grenade per wave, table flips; squads, bodyguards, VIP), `src/sim/waves.ts` spawn points + wave scripts (entries take `SpawnOpts`: variant, squad, unaware placement), `src/data/stages/` room and wave data. Spawn variety (`TUNING.waves.variety`, `on: 0` = the old trickle): the `WaveDirector` cuts every non-`exact` wave into seeded groups (2-3), spreads them over the room's spawn points (both sides, clear of the players), sends an occasional flanker from behind the player (event `flank`: whistle + HUD chevron in `stageHud.ts`), and hurries the next group when almost nobody is left; `tests/spawns.test.ts` (`PROBE=1` prints the before/after report).
- M9: `src/sim/stage.ts` (stages as room graphs: one-way exits edge/door/ladder/vent/breach/lift/fall/climb, entries walk/above/vault, traversal + ink-wipe events, `stageRunner` = a sequencer script, checkpoint per room via `roomEnter`). Stage 2 data and room scripts: `src/data/stages/stage2.ts`, `stage2Script.ts` (target, riot-helmet mini-boss, the boss's cameo: fling + mass raise). Scenery: `src/render/canvas/sceneryPainter.ts` (night street, rooftops, ladder, breach mark, ceiling holes, crates); wipe + tracker: `src/render/stageHud.ts`.
- M12: `src/sim/arena.ts` (the arena lock: walls drop after their shadows and crush, `room.lockWalls`, sealed = indoor; the last stand: `world.lastStand`, players aim and fire only for ~5 s), the zombie mini-boss's sign armour (`Actor.damageScale`), the vending machine prop (thrown bodies smash it), the boss's sword dive and teleport and the stab (`src/data/stages/stage3Late.ts`: desert, vending, console, lift rooms).
- M11: `src/sim/improbability.ts` (improbability mode: `world.physics`, knockback x2.4, wall/ceiling bounces, ceiling sticks, enemies flying in; the arena collapse: `world.collapse`, `room.void`; falling blocks: the tank), hovering actors (`Actor.hover`, the jetpack), the vending-machine brute (`variant: 'vending'`), the sky-fall entry. Stage 3 data/scripts: `src/data/stages/stage3.ts`, `stage3Script.ts`. Axe throws are a room rule (`world.flags` 'axeThrows'). The mixer has a tape wobble (`setWobble`) for improbability mode.
- M10: `src/sim/elevator.ts` (the cab: roof deck with hatch/slot holes via `RoomDef.decks`, welder cut, cables breakable only after the ambush and from the roof, fall + crash), `src/sim/interrogate.ts` (hold-up of the last unarmed enemy, pictogram interrogation, knockout), dazed knockdowns (`dazeOnDefeat`), in-room warp exits (the crate room's vent). Late stage 2 scripts: `src/data/stages/stage2Late.ts` (elevator, swords, lobby, crates, final office + the ending).
- M7: `src/sim/stealth.ts` (unaware/alert rules, E takedowns from behind, drop kills from above), `src/sim/cover.ts` (kicked-over tables), `src/sim/zombies.ts` (raise rule, rise, zombie damage, headless). Co-op guests set `world.puppet` so replayed events don't re-run sim rules.
- M8: `src/sim/sequencer.ts` (`world.seq`): generator scripts for intros/cutscenes/set pieces, stepped first in `World.step`. They yield seconds or a condition and use `wait/until/join/moveTo/act/steer/lockInput/spawn/place/giveItem/drop/card/cameraShake/music/waves/clear`. Scripted commands (`world.scripted`) override brains and player input; `card`/`music`/`cameraShake` are events (HUD, `MusicSystem.setTrack`, feel). Stage 1 NPCs: `variant` 'boombox' / 'dancer' (placed without AI, `src/render/canvas/npcPainter.ts`, dance layer in `src/anim/layers.ts`); the boombox is `actor.carry` and a floor item, `locked` until the ending; then E (or walking over it) carries it in the front hand and nothing else is picked up meanwhile. The ending's dance emote is `pose.dancing` (same loop as the dancer). Boss: `src/sim/boss.ts` (kind 'boss', `actor.boss` state machine stepped instead of stepActor; entrance, lightning raise, ward checked in `hitscan.castRay`, `bossHit` from applyHit; one `act` at a time: cast raise/strike, dash → grab → slams, phase 2 sweep → summon → volley), `src/render/canvas/bossPainter.ts`, lab `/?lab=boss-halo`. His cannon is a `world.cannons` entry with `live` (balls hurt, `src/sim/cannon.ts`: `dropCannon` shadow → fall → crush, `placeCannon`, `removeCannon`) plus a `cannon` prop (solid, standable, never cover). Music cues: the script's `music(cue)` → `MusicSystem.setCue` → the room's `cues` in `assets/music/cues.json`; the boombox plays the room's `diegetic` track (`src/audio/boombox.ts`, radio static until delivered). Wave B's cannoneer: `SpawnOpts.role: 'cannon'` → `cannonStep` in `src/sim/ai.ts`; a manned cannon fires only while its gunner is able.
- M14b A1 sim stability (`tests/stability.test.ts`): `removeActor(world, a)` in `world.ts` is the only way to take an actor out mid-room (scripts, swept bodies, the void): it unsticks blades, clears holders and emits `actorRemoved { actorId }`. Room rules live on the room (`StageRoom.rules: { physics, flags }`, `applyRoomRules` in `enterRoom`; stage 3: improbable street, `axeThrows` from the camera room on). `resetForRoom(actor)` in `stage.ts` clears transient action state on entry (a cooked grenade goes back; `clearInputBuffer` is the hook for slice B's buffer); a hero killed on the exit stays dead. Each room reseeds `world.rng` from `roomSeed(stage, room, entry, world.attempt)` (saved as `world.roomSeed`; `stageRunner(def, room, entry, seed)` takes it back) and restarts the world's id counter (`bindIds`, `World.ids`). The soak: `tests/soak.test.ts` (always: every room, masher, 20 s; `SOAK=1` the full run) on `tests/soakHarness.ts`; invariants in `src/sim/invariants.ts`.
- Labs: `/?lab` lists them, `/?lab=<slug>` opens one (`src/labs/registry.ts`). Every mechanic gets a replica lab compared against its reference frames.

## Co-op (2 laptops, same Wi-Fi)
Host: `npm run dev:lan` (or `preview_start` "dev-lan"), then on the title screen Co-op (host) and pick a stage (or Continue from your checkpoint); `/?coop=host&stage=N` does the same. Read the join link it shows. Player 2 opens that link (`http://<host-ip>:5173/?coop=join`) in Chrome or Safari. The host's laptop runs the game (`src/net/`); player 2's laptop sends inputs and draws snapshots. Single player stays at `/`.

## Controls
Esc pause menu (keys can be rebound in Settings → Controls) · WASD move (W/S depth) · Space jump · Ctrl or C dive/prone (WASD crawls on the belly, guns still fire forward), again or Space to get up (dive into an adjacent enemy = low sweep; dive in mid-air = flying kick, knocks down the first enemy it meets) · mouse aims (facing follows it) · LMB light punch · RMB heavy punch (cursor steeply above the shoulder = uppercut; a punch at a downed body = finisher) · F grab (both hands empty): the nearest within ~1.1 H on either side, living enemies before bodies, and an F pressed mid-move fires when the move ends; F (or a punch) again throws toward the cursor · with a gun: LMB fires the front hand, RMB the back hand (hold for full auto), empty → click → press again to throw it · E pick up (or pull a stuck blade / the planted sign out, 0.5 s); with both hands full tap E swaps the worse hand, hold E + LMB/RMB picks the hand (M17); walking over a loaded gun fills an empty hand · R reload · Q throw the held weapon · Shift (hold) split aim · Tab with a full rage bar = rage fatality (M19) · G hold to cook a grenade, release to lob it at the floor point under the cursor · melee weapon in hand: LMB swings (knife: stabs up close, thrown at range), long weapon: LMB sweep, RMB thrust (pins against walls/poles) · F on an armed grunt steals his weapon · F just before a thrown weapon arrives catches it, and F with a free hand snatches any weapon flying within reach of your hands, your own included (e.g. a gun bouncing back off a wall) · F on a live grenade picks it up, F/LMB throws it back · F on a grunt with a one-handed gun in the back hand = human shield, RMB fires past it · M10b combos: LMB to stun, then RMB with the cursor high launches him; LMB jabs keep him up (max 3), RMB spikes him (or kills him: head punt), F grabs him out of the air · heavies that slam a grunt into a wall/prop bounce him back for a juggle · a punched-out weapon pops up: F catches it · an empty gun thrown at a head stuns (one heavy then kills) · with a gun, RMB at an enemy in contact behind you = over-the-shoulder shot · E at a ladder / in a lift / under the elevator hatch / under a vent: climb or ride (a room exit) · E next to a surrendered enemy: interrogate · E behind an unaware enemy = silent takedown (wire 1.5 s with empty hands, knife 0.6 s otherwise); landing next to one from above = knife kill.

Co-op moves (M18): hit a grunt your partner holds (3rd jab or any heavy kills) · F on it = wishbone · both hitting one enemy within 0.25 s = instant kill · a body knocked into your partner bounces back (heavy bats it to die at the wall) · heavy at a body your partner threw = body bat · Q with the cursor on your partner = pass · F with the cursor on your partner (hands empty) = lift, F/LMB throws them, Space hops off; carried, F = carry-spin (shoot to your side only; the carrier's F throws you out).

Downed in co-op (waiting for a revive): WASD drags you along slowly.

Every action also works mid-jump (punch, shoot, swing, throw, cook/lob a grenade); the jump carries on underneath, and punches/swings from the air come down on whoever is below. Not in the air: grabbing, picking up, pulling out, stealing (and you can't jump while carrying a body).

## Debug keys
F1 overlay (H grid, rulers, hurtboxes, tick/fps) · F2 slow-mo 0.25× · F3 pause · F4 or `.` step one tick · 1/2/3 stage palettes (also blood: stage 1 has none) · P post FX on/off · K drop 20 corpses · V effect intensity (full/reduced/off) · M mute · L drop a random gun at each player's feet · Z raise every intact corpse as a zombie. · F6 (dev builds) tuning panel: every number in TUNING/FEEL/GUNS/GRENADE/THROWN/DISARM, live, saved in localStorage across reloads (`src/dev/`), "Copy changes" to write them back into `src/data/`.

Dev only: `window.__game` is the Phaser game. The browser pane pauses animation frames while it is hidden; drive the loop with `__game.step(t, 16.7)` from the console when checking things there.

## Rules
- Measured values follow the show; invented values are marked `// invented` and stay tunable.
- Every mechanic ships with its sound and VFX (milestone "Look & sound" lists).
- New character types get a character-sheet entry in the style frame first, in our own design.
- Never commit reference footage. Sound files need an entry in `assets/audio/LICENSES.md`. Ask the user before downloading asset packs.
