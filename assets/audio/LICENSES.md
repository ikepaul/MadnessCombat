## Generated in code

Sounds without a recording (whooshes, enemy vocalisations, ambiences, the kill-cam tape stop, the bone crack layer) are synthesized at startup by `src/audio/synth.ts` (noise, sine sweeps and filters; no samples, nothing taken from the episodes). Ids and recipes are listed in `src/data/audio.ts`. These are our own work and need no licence entry; they also stand in while recordings decode.
